Our Story

Rooted in Indian craftsmanship, designed for contemporary homes.

Craftsmanship Meets Everyday Warmth

Artisan Decor Studio began with a simple idea: home decor should feel personal, tactile, and enduring. We partner directly with independent metalworkers, potters, and lighting studios to create small-batch collections.

Every lamp, mirror, and accent piece is inspected by hand before leaving our workshop, ensuring clean finishes and lasting durability without unnecessary middlemen.
